I am Abdulrahman Ghanem, a multidisciplinary engineer. Currently, pursuing an M.Eng. in Computer Engineering (modeling and simulation concentration) at Minia university. I am interested in control systems, ML, automation, and software engineering. I am proficient at many programming languages but during my degree, I have been using Julia exclusively for the college stuff.

Being a big fan of open-source software I try to contribute to the community as much as I can. I was lucky that I could contribute to [kitspace.org](https://master.staging.kitspace.dev/) and [EDeA](https://gitlab.com/edea-dev/) (funded by [nlnet](https://nlnet.nl/project/ElectronicsHub)) as part of my previous roles. As part of my effort to spread the open-source culture, I founded #El-Garash, a community for CSE department students at Minia in which we mentor students and help them to contribute to open-source projects and solve real world problems.
