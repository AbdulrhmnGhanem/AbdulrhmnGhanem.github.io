## My shallow blueprint for replicating Bell Labs

1. Find a (really) wicked problem
2. Start a business around it
3. Become profitable to some extent that you can afford to do fundamental research
4. Give your researchers the freedom they want
5. Free the researchers from all burdens (funding, marketing, manufacturing) except for a pressing urge to solve hard problems
6. Become even more profitable, and invest more in research
7. repeat the cycle
8. Eliminate competition, not by becoming a monopoly, but rather by making all people around you win to the greatest possible extent
9. Eventually, it will degrade, make sure that happens gracefully.
